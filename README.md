# ⚽ Footballers

**A full-body physics ragdoll football game that runs in your browser.** Every stride, first touch, curled finish and slide tackle is muscles, joints and momentum, simulated 400 times a second.

[![Visit the website](https://img.shields.io/badge/%E2%9C%A8%20WEBSITE-3ef0a5?style=for-the-badge&labelColor=04070a)](https://dashryerson-design.github.io/Footballers/)
[![Play in browser](https://img.shields.io/badge/%E2%96%B6%20PLAY%20NOW-36c9ff?style=for-the-badge&labelColor=04070a)](https://dashryerson-design.github.io/Footballers/play/)

**Current version: Alpha 8.0** · see the full patch log on the website or in the game's PATCH NOTES menu.

### What's in it
- **Active ragdolls**: 19-body players with PD muscles, balance, falls and get-ups
- **Close control**: predictive possession assist, shielding, skill moves (spin, side boost, flick)
- **Shooting**: charged whole-body strikes, finesse curlers, trajectory preview
- **Passing**: charged ground passes and lobs, through balls, call for the ball
- **Defending**: standing and slide tackles, player collisions, knockdowns
- **AI**: a teammate who finds space, defenders who hold shape and carry the ball, a diving keeper
- **Goals**: FIFA-style frames with cloth-simulated nets
- **Scenarios, settings, touch controls**, and physics detail levels for phones

### Controls
**Keyboard:** WASD move · Shift sprint · hold LMB shoot / RMB finesse · E pass (hold to charge, or call for it) · R lob · Z / X / C skill moves · without the ball: LMB tackle, E slide · 1 reset · P pause
**Touch:** joystick, SHOOT/TACKLE, PASS/SLIDE, LOB, FINESSE, SPRINT, Z/X/C, reset and pause buttons
