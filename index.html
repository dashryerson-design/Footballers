EVERY RELEASE

Patch log

110 releases so far, from the first ragdoll on an empty pitch to a full career. Newest first.

Alpha 11.0Career: the gameplay camera now always comes back when you take control in the combine drills (passing, finishing) - before, the cinematic camera stayed on.
Alpha 10.9A world to play in: six distinct venues built around the pitch. The career opening now happens in a fenced tarmac street cage at sunset, among flats, graffiti and streetlights. The scouting combine (arrival, all three challenges and the showcase match) is in a big, near-empty two-tier stadium at night under floodlights, with the six clubs' scouts at branded tables along the touchline. Club matches are played at a venue that fits the home club: a community ground with a rail, clubhouse, trees and houses (tier 4), a lower-league ground with a covered main stand, terraces and floodlights (tier 3), a big professional bowl (tier 2) or a full modern stadium with LED ribbons and big screens (tier 1). The signing is now three real sets: the club's training-ground exterior (glass, concrete, club colours, crest and name over the entrance), a branded corridor and a changing room with named lockers, where the kit manager hands over your shirt.
Alpha 10.8Career Chapter 1 - Humble Beginnings. Confirming a new player now opens the story: the chapter title, your first goal on a Sunday-league pitch (slow-motion build-up, then you take the shot under pressure; miss and it rewinds to the pass), the coach noticing you, an invitation to a scouting combine, three challenges (technical control through cones, four passes, four finishing chances against a keeper), a 4-minute showcase match, then six lower-league clubs grade you independently (A+ to F) by their own recruitment style and your position. Pick from the contract offers (always as a substitute, with an allocated position and a weekly wage), watch the signing and the shirt reveal with your name and squad number, and your career hub shows your club. Career matches are now played for your club. Progress is saved at every stage.
Alpha 10.7Headers: plant and jump straight up instead of flying along with the ball.
Alpha 10.6Aerial drills fixed: defenders stand off, and the delivery counts as your ball so you can shoot.
Alpha 10.5Practice: 8 new aerial drills in the scenario list - Volley: Cross from Left / Right, Dropping Ball, Side-On; Header: Cross from Left / Right, Leap for It, Clear It. The ball is delivered to you a second after the reset and the drill repeats by itself (T resets any time). Also fixes volleys that whiffed when standing still (the kicking foot was being pinned to the turf), headers now get across to the ball better, and balls above waist-to-chest height go to headers instead of jumping volleys.
Alpha 10.4Jumping headers. One aerial check now decides feet or head: if the ball can be kicked, it's a volley; if it will reach him at head height first, or only his head can get there, he heads it; if neither can reach, nothing is forced. Reach comes from each player's real build (standing head height from his height) plus what his Jumping rating adds, so tall, springy players win more high balls. The header is physical: he gets under it, dips, takes off on time, arches and snaps forward, and only genuine head-ball contact counts. Heading Accuracy sets direction and cleanness. Same shoot button (the bar reads HEADER); teammates told to shoot head it when it's too high to volley; AI on both sides heads at goal near the box, to a teammate in midfield, or clears it near their own goal.
Alpha 10.3Volleys: hold shoot while the ball is in the air and release to strike it first time. The flight is predicted from the ball's real velocity and gravity, the player moves into position, raises the leg to the ball's actual height and side (knee-high, waist-high, side-on or a jumping volley) and the boot is driven onto it; the shot only happens on a real foot-ball contact, and how cleanly it's caught sets the power and accuracy. Release too early or too late and you mis-hit or miss. Volleys rating = technique and accuracy, Jumping = reach and timing forgiveness. Tell a teammate to shoot while your lob is still in the air and he volleys it; AI players on both sides can volley aerial balls near goal.
Alpha 10.2Snappier again: a commanded teammate shot now goes off in ~0.1-0.6 s (he no longer has to turn perfectly square to goal first, and the shoot command works however long he's had the ball); light passes have more zip (arrive at 8.5 m/s instead of 6); short, through and lobbed passes from teammates measured at 0.1-0.95 s from the command.
Alpha 10.1Faster game: fully charged ground passes now fly (up to ~30 m/s off the boot, still ~19 m/s on arrival, reach up to 56 m) while taps stay short and soft; lobs reach further. AI players decide and act in a fraction of a second - opposition carriers pass in ~0.2-0.9 s, teammates release passes sooner the closer the pressure and can pass on the move, shooters strike without waiting to be perfectly square. Plus a new team brain: 40 tactical strategies (each club gets one matching its identity, changeable on the Quick Match rules page), one presser at a time, covering, lane blocking, runners, overlaps and box occupation.
Alpha 10.0Free kicks and kick-ins rebuilt as proper set pieces: play stops, the ball is pinned to its spot (no player can nudge it), and both teams reorganise - the side taking it into its attacking shape with short, medium and forward options, the other side into a compact defensive shape, with a two-man wall for central free kicks in range. Everyone except the taker stays at least 8 metres from the ball; players run to their spots, and anyone far away is helped along smoothly rather than teleported. You take your side's set pieces with the normal controls - shoot, E pass or R lofted ball - and their nearest player takes theirs, shooting from range or picking a pass. Play goes live the instant the ball is kicked
Alpha 9.9Quick Match: after picking teams and kits you now choose which outfield starter you control - forward, either midfielder or the defender - and the other three play their own roles under the AI. At half-time a Switch Player button lets you take over a different team-mate for the second half; the score, events and every player's stats carry over
Alpha 9.8A bigger, more international football world: 40 clubs from 14 countries (England 6; Spain, Germany, Italy and France 4 each; the Netherlands and Portugal 3; Scotland, Belgium, Turkey, Brazil and Argentina 2; Austria and Denmark 1) with 280 players. 16 brand-new clubs - including Madrid Altura, Milano Sovrana and Paris Lumière among the elite - each with a hand-drawn crest, its own colour palette, two kits and a full squad; ten former English clubs have moved abroad with their crests. Every one of the 1,560 possible fixtures gets a clear kit contrast
Alpha 9.7Cleaner team selection: compact club panels (crest, name, starting-five rating, who you play as), a simple VS mark instead of the comparison bars, slimmer carousels showing three clubs either side with crests only and the selected club named, and a phone layout that keeps both 3D players in frame
Alpha 9.6Quick Match is now a three-step build-up: pick your club and your opponent from 3D cover-flow carousels (crest, squad and starting-five ratings, a head-to-head comparison of both line-ups), then choose each team's kit (with a live clash check and auto-pick), then set half length and difficulty and kick off - all in front of a 3D stage where each club's player turns on a glowing plinth in the chosen kit, lit in the club's colours. The Squads menu (tester teams) is gone
Alpha 9.5Clubs & Rosters: 24 original clubs (16 English, plus Spain, Germany, Italy, France, the Netherlands, Portugal and Belgium) in four tiers, 168 registered players with their own names, nationalities, numbers, archetypes, ratings, bodies and looks, and a hand-drawn crest for every club. Home and away kits with stripes, hoops, halves, sashes, pinstripes, chevrons and chest bands, with automatic clash avoidance. Browse every club - crest, kits, squad / starting five / bench averages, the 1-2-1 line-up on a pitch, the bench and full player profiles with the 3D model in club kit. Quick Match now picks two clubs: their five starters take the pitch with their real attributes, bodies, kits and club tactics (pressing, width); substitutes stay on the team sheet
Alpha 9.4Possession transitions: each team now has a steady attack / defend phase that only flips when the other side genuinely takes control (held for a quarter of a second) - passes, through balls, lofted balls and loose balls never trigger it. Winning the ball: the forward's run starts at once, the near midfielder steps ahead of the ball, the far one runs into the space beyond and the defender pushes up behind. Losing it: players caught upfield sprint back goal-side of the ball on paths that bend round it, the team drops off into a compact block (marking is picked up once it is set), one man presses and nobody else chases
Alpha 9.3Passing and width: teammates show for the ball where a pass can actually reach them (support spots swing round the carrier until the lane is clear of opponents), hold still while you shape to pass, and the intended receiver goes to meet it; ground passes travel firmer. A pass you brush with your follow-through is no longer lost from the stats. The far midfielder now always holds the opposite flank, the near one and the defender spread wider, players sprint to their spots sooner and their targets glide instead of zig-zagging
Alpha 9.25v5 matches: each side now fields a keeper, a defender, two midfielders and a forward (you take your own role; Practice keeps the 1 v 2 test pitch). A shared team-shape system gives every AI player a job: in possession the defender stays as the safe ball back, one midfielder offers a short angle (closer when you are pressed or facing back), the other takes the pocket ahead on the far side, and the forward holds the last line in the widest gap and times diagonal runs when the carrier faces forward with time; out of possession the team drops into a compact block, one man presses and the rest mark goal-side. The opposition uses the same system and picks its best open passing option. New match HUD: broadcast scoreboard with a goal pulse, possession strip, your player chip (number, name, position, OVR, stamina) and marker, an event feed for passes, tackles, interceptions, skill moves, goals, assists and possession changes, and labelled shot / pass charge bars
Alpha 9.1Career: delete a player save from the Career hub (with a confirmation). Your next saved player takes over, or Career returns to the creator if none are left
Alpha 9.0Archetype cards rebuilt: every archetype is now a brand - THE EXECUTIONER, THE PREDATOR, THE ARCHITECT, THE WALL and 21 more - each with its own hand-drawn emblem, colour palette, background pattern and tagline. Angular cut-corner cards with bold condensed names, a watermark crest, the archetype's three strongest categories drawn from its real starting ratings with ceiling ticks, and OVR / potential; a detail panel shows the full breakdown. Saved players and progression are unchanged
Alpha 8.9Career rework: create your player from any archetype with no position lock, then manage him in a Career hub (Overview, Position, Progression, Player Details). Pick and change position any time without touching archetype, ratings or progress; any player can play in goal with his own goalkeeping ratings. Earn XP from completed passes, forward passes, skill moves that keep the ball, shots on target, goals, assists, tackles, interceptions, saves, wins, clean sheets and Player of the Match. Levels give 6 attribute points, every 5th level 11; spend them up to your archetype ceilings with a live OVR preview. Old created players are upgraded automatically
Alpha 8.8Career: Create Player now lives inside Career mode (no separate menu option). Career opens the creator for your first player; once you have one it opens your player, with a button to create another
Alpha 8.7Create Player: a 7-step creator (biometrics, customisation, identity, position, archetype, attributes, review) with a live 3D preview built from the same body code as the match. Height, weight, wingspan and frame rebuild your actual ragdoll - its size, proportions, mass, reach and collision boxes - and weight changes acceleration and turning. 25 archetypes (21 outfield, 4 goalkeeper-only) each set starting ratings, separate rating ceilings and a working PlayStyle; potential OVR from the ceilings. Blocky hair, facial hair, face presets, accessories, boots and socks; name and number on the shirt; squad numbers stay unique. Saved players appear in SQUADS and can be switched or deleted
Alpha 8.6Player attributes: every footballer has EA FC-style ratings (29 outfield attributes in PAC / SHO / PAS / DRI / DEF / PHY, or 6 goalkeeper-only ones) with a position-weighted OVR, shown on new SQUADS cards and detail screens. They drive the game: acceleration, sprint speed, agility and stamina in the movement controller; strength and balance in knock-downs; shot power, finishing, long shots, volleys, penalties, curve and composure in every strike; passing and crossing accuracy; ball control and dribbling in first touches and carrying; tackling, interceptions, awareness, aggression and reactions in the AI; diving, handling, reflexes, speed and positioning for keepers. Difficulty now only changes AI decisions
Alpha 8.5AI shooting and goal attribution: opposition players read shooting chances (distance, angle, open lanes, keeper position, pressure, a better-placed partner) and shoot with a turned body and a swung leg; Easy / Normal / Hard change range, decisions, placement and the odd speculative strike. Press shoot while your teammate is receiving or holding your pass and he shoots. Goals: possession and shots now count as touches, restarts clear the last touch, shots deflected in credit the shooter, own goals only for a team putting it in its own net
Alpha 8.4Complete match engine: both teams now have a goalkeeper (one shared keeper system, mirrored per goal); proper penalties for either side (spot kick setup, everyone else held outside the box, AI or user taker, saves and rebounds live); keeper saves counted from any body block and removed if parried in; Enter on the home screen opens the mode select
Alpha 8.3Full matches: Quick Match mode with kick-off countdowns, timed halves, goals, kick-ins, corners, goal kicks and fouls; live scoreboard, half-time and full-time reports with team and player stats, ratings and Player of the Match; saved match history and career totals
