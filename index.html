159 releases so far, from the first ragdoll on an empty pitch to a full career. Newest first.

Alpha 16.0Stadium fix: the crowd no longer spills over the pitch corners in the rounded bowls (Global Cup, London, Sydney, New York, Paris, Milan, Abu Dhabi, Tokyo); every bowl now clears the corner flags by at least 3 m. All 12 stadiums have a solid perimeter wall with a glass rail between the pitch and the front row.
Alpha 15.9Quick Match fixed: KICK OFF now starts the match even where the browser blocks session storage (embedded players, private modes). Careers, resumes and the world fixtures use the same fallback.
Alpha 15.812 new stadiums, each with its own city, country and architecture: Global Cup Stadium (every Global Club Championship match), Metropolitan Arena (London), The Foundry (Dortmund), Coastline (Sydney), Royal Park (Brussels), Skyline Arena (New York), Stade National (Paris), The Citadel (Milan), Horizon (Barcelona), Summit Arena (Abu Dhabi), Riverside Ground (Amsterdam), Nova Stadium (Tokyo). Every club plays at its nearest stadium; away games are at the home club's ground. Live big screens show the score, clock and scorers, and a cinematic establishing shot opens each match (Space / tap skips).
Alpha 15.7Typing in the sign-in / create-account form (or any text box) no longer moves the menus or triggers game keys. Esc closes the account panel.
Alpha 15.6Accounts are live: sign in from the ACCOUNT button to keep your careers in the cloud.
Alpha 15.5Footballers accounts: create an account or sign in with email and password (the ACCOUNT button, top right of the menus) and your careers, career worlds, settings and match history are saved to it automatically and follow you to any device. If a device and your account both have saves you choose which to keep; signing out keeps a copy on the device.
Alpha 15.4The world's stars now put up huge numbers in simulated games, so awards are a real fight: 86+ OVR players take a far bigger share of their side's goals and assists, 88+ attackers find extra goals of their own, elite defenders and keepers dominate the tackles, interceptions and saves columns, and star ratings are higher. In a test season the top scorer hit 30 goals in 18 games and the Player of the Year went 23 goals, 17 assists, 9.27 average. Your own player's numbers still come only from the matches you play.
Alpha 15.3Ratings now decide the football. Every attribute runs through a curve where below 65 is clearly poor and 90+ is elite: top speed (a 55-pace player sprints about 5.8 m/s, a 95 about 8.2), acceleration (time to reach that speed), agility (turning), strength and balance in contact, stamina, reactions, control, dribbling, composure, and the keeper's reflexes, diving, handling, positioning and speed. Shooting: accuracy falls away steeply with low Finishing / Long Shots (a 55 is several times less accurate than a 90), Shot Power sets the real ball speed, Curve limits the bend, and every player has a range set by Long Shots and Shot Power - beyond it the strike loses accuracy and pace, so a 75/75 rookie can no longer score from halfway (that now takes roughly 90+ in both). Passing: Short / Long Passing and Crossing set the accuracy and pace of each kind of ball, and Vision now decides how much help you get - from a wide target cone, aim correction, steering in flight and leading runners round defenders at high Vision, to almost none at low Vision (you aim it yourself). The same curves apply to AI players.
Alpha 15.2Career menus laid out so everything is readable: upcoming fixtures and TBC games wrap onto new rows instead of running off the screen, leaderboard and award-race names show in full (no more initials), competition and offer cards wrap, the tab bar wraps on desktop, and on phones every career page now fits the screen width.
Alpha 15.1Career mode now opens on the HOME dashboard: who you play next (date, venue, what is at stake) with a PLAY NEXT MATCH button, your competitions and where you stand in them, the league-phase table, statistical leaders, award watch and the news feed. Your player sheet (OVR, attributes, level) moves to the PLAYER tab.
Alpha 15.0Story cutscenes can be skipped: a small SPACE · SKIP prompt sits in the bottom-right corner during every Career story cutscene (TAP TO SKIP on touch controls). Skipping fast-forwards the scene to its end and stops by itself as soon as you get control or a choice, so nothing in the story is missed or broken.
Alpha 14.9Goal replays can be skipped: tap or click SKIP REPLAY (bottom right), or press Space, Enter or Esc, and it goes straight on to the kick-off.
Alpha 14.8Career opening shot: you now take the pass and have the ball at your feet before control is handed to you, a clear prompt tells you to shoot and how (Shoot using Left Click, or Shoot using the SHOOT button on touch), and the beaten defenders drop well away so nobody can tackle you or block the ball while it is your shot.
Alpha 14.7The career opening is a game again. The street-cage build-up was leaving players standing around between camera shots; now every player always has somewhere to be: your team-mate drives forward while you make a run, the defender comes out to press him and he slips it into your stride, the defender turns and chases you, you play a one-two back into his run, the last man steps across and is beaten (a beat of slow motion), and you arrive at the edge of the box for the final pass - each moment starts the instant the last one finishes. The pass comes straight to you with no caption pause, and the shot is yours as before. The combine showcase match is now 2 minutes.
Alpha 14.6Broadcast overlays that stay put. Entrances and exits are slower and the numbers count up only once the panel is fully in.
Alpha 14.5Career intro, directed. The street-cage opening is now a choreographed move: your team-mate carries it, plays you in, you lay it back into his run, he holds off a late challenge (a beat of slow motion) and plays the final ball to your feet as the camera hands over to you - then the shot is yours, with the normal controls and keeper. Alpha 14.4Goal replays rebuilt and goals explained. The shot is captured the moment it is struck, so the replay shows real numbers even after the kick-off reset: distance to goal, the angle between the posts, ball speed in km/h, expected goals (from distance, angle and the defenders in the way), shot type, inside or outside the area, where it crossed the line, the nearest defender, a shot map with the keeper and defenders, the shooter's finishing / shot power / long shots attributes, and labels like LONG RANGE, FIRST-TIME FINISH, NARROW ANGLE, POWERFUL STRIKE, TOP CORNER and VOLLEY only when the goal earns them. New replay camera work with proper pacing: a high establishing shot, low side-on tracking, a low swooping ball-tracker that leaves room for the analysis, a front-facing shot as he runs at the lens, behind the shooter, a slow-motion close-up on the strike, an ultra-low ball camera for powerful shots, behind the keeper, a slow-motion goal-line shot and a wide finish - every goal shot runs on after the ball crosses the line, and slow motion is kept to the strike and the goal. Panels move to the other side, or step out, when the ball or a player comes into them. The celebration card now builds in (name, club and position, then OVR, goals today, season and career goals, then match stats), counts up, marks real milestones (first career goal, first goal for a club, hat-tricks, 10th / 25th / 50th career goal) and moves aside if the scorer is on that side of the frame.

Alpha 14.1Career reputation, recruitability, contracts and transfers. Your reputation (Unknown Prospect up to Superstar) is earned in real matches - goals, assists, tackles, saves, clean sheets, high ratings, Player of the Match, wins - worth more against stronger clubs and in bigger competitions, and trophies add a lot; it fades a little if you stop playing. Clubs judge you on Recruitability: 70% your OVR, 30% your reputation, against their own standard (squad strength and club reputation), and never sign someone more than 8 OVR below their squad, however famous. Position and squad depth matter. Clubs move from monitoring to interested to an offer, mostly in the transfer windows. The new CONTRACTS tab shows your contract, reputation and the clubs watching you, lets you compare, accept or reject offers, request a transfer or approach clubs yourself (with sensible limits), and keeps your career history. Transfers keep everything you have earned and your competitions follow your new club. AI clubs recruit too: every summer stronger clubs sign better players from weaker ones.

Alpha 12.0Attribute audit: all 35 ratings (29 outfield, 6 goalkeeper) were traced into gameplay and the weak ones strengthened. Sprint Speed now spans about 6.4 m/s at 50 to 8.3 m/s at 90 (was a 16% spread); Strength (with Balance) now helps you ride a tackle and keep the ball, makes your own tackles more likely to win it against weaker carriers and lets strong AI carriers hold the ball under a challenge; Free Kick Accuracy now governs direct free kicks (yours and the AI's); Shot Power, Aggression and Dribbling have wider, more noticeable ranges; Strength + Balance weigh more in knock-downs. Alpha 11.9Slide tackles reworked. The slide now carries your own running speed plus a small push (about 3-7 m/s) instead of a fixed burst, and the turf's friction slows you through it, so you no longer travel miles or gain speed by chaining slides. The body is no longer a limp rag: muscles keep some tone, the torso and hips are held steady and leaning back, legs lead to the ball, arms go out for balance, and twisting and spinning are damped - but it is still a ragdoll that gives under contact. Collisions are untouched: the slider still hits the ball, the opponent and gets hit back. AI slides follow the same rules.
Alpha 11.8Goalkeeper playability overhaul. Fast shots can no longer slip through a keeper's hands, arms or body: the ball's path over every physics step is now checked against each keeper limb and the torso, and if it crossed one it bounces off that part (real contact, no invisible wall). The keeper's feet are no longer pinned to the turf during a dive - that was anchoring him in place - so dives now travel. A SMART DIVE ASSIST runs when you're in goal: you choose when and which way; it reads the shot (where and how high it will cross, and when), corrects a slightly wrong direction, launches him far and high enough, steers the body through the air so the near glove meets the ball on time, and brings both hands and the shoulders to the ball (low ones get a lower, longer dive; high ones a big leap with arms up). Shots straight at you become a standing save instead of a dive. Diving and Agility set reach, speed and how much it can correct; it isn't perfect (a small per-dive error), and a late or wrong-way dive still gets beaten. You're back on your feet faster for rebounds.
