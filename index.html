THE GAME

Built from physics up

Everything you see is simulated. Here's what's in the game so far.

Active ragdolls

Every player is a 19-body ragdoll driven by PD muscles, foot planting and balance. They stride, lean, fall and get back up for real.

  • 400 HZ
  • MUSCLE TONE
  • GET-UPS

Close control

A predictive possession system keeps the ball at your feet while you run, turn and weave, and only a real tackle takes it off you.

  • CARRY ASSIST
  • SHIELDING
  • PRESSURE AWARE

Shooting

A charged, whole-body strike. Power shots, curled finesse finishes, camera-height loft and a trajectory preview with a glowing ball trail.

  • FINESSE
  • CURVE
  • AIM GUIDE

Passing

Charged ground passes and lobs with smart assistance that leads runners into space. Call for the ball, or hold to ask for a through ball.

  • CHARGE-UP
  • LOBS
  • THROUGH BALLS

Skill moves

Spin roulette, a side-boost burst and a running flick, all done with real foot-to-ball contact.

  • Z SPIN
  • X SIDE BOOST
  • C FLICK

Tackles & collisions

Lunge in with a standing tackle or go to ground with a controlled slide. Players collide, and big hits can take their legs away.

  • STANDING
  • SLIDING
  • IMPACTS

Football AI

A teammate who finds space, makes runs and wins loose balls. Defenders who hold their shape, carry the ball and pass. A keeper who dives for real.

  • TEAMMATE
  • DEFENDERS
  • KEEPER

Real goals

FIFA-style frames with rear supports and a cloth-simulated net that bulges, ripples and catches the ball, with an impact flash on every finish.

  • CLOTH NET
  • RIPPLES
  • IMPACT FX

Scenarios

Instant drills: free kicks, run-ons, one-on-ones, long range, wide angles and a full defending scenario. Reset with one key.

  • 13 PRESETS
  • DEFEND
  • INSTANT RESET

Mobile ready

Full touch controls, physics detail levels, smart render scaling and a settings menu, so it runs on phones as well as desktops.

  • TOUCH UI
  • PHYSICS LOD
  • SETTINGS
